Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared with ESR Corporate Consultants LLP through this website.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ESR Corporate Consultants LLP ("ESR", "we", "us") handles personal information in connection with this website and our enquiry process. By using this website you acknowledge this policy.

Information We May Collect

  • Details you choose to share when you contact us, such as your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • Documents and information you choose to share with us if you engage us for a service.
  • Basic technical information that web browsers and hosting services commonly record, such as browser type, device type and pages visited.

How The Enquiry Form Works

The enquiry form on our contact page opens your email application with your details pre-filled, addressed to info@esrcorporateconsultants.com. The website itself does not store what you type into the form. The message is only sent when you send the email from your own email application.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and understand your requirement.
  • To provide the services you ask us to provide and communicate with you about them.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and professional record-keeping obligations that apply to us.

Sharing Of Information

We use your information for the purpose for which you shared it. Where a service requires it, information may need to be submitted to government portals, authorities or other parties involved in completing that service, or disclosed where required by law.

Third-Party Services And Links

This website links to third-party services such as WhatsApp and Google Maps. When you use them, their own terms and privacy practices apply, and we do not control them.

Data Security And Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information that is shared with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We keep information for as long as needed for the purpose it was provided, or as required by applicable law or professional obligations.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct or request removal of the personal information you have shared with us. We will consider such requests subject to any legal or record-keeping requirements that apply.

Children

This website is intended for business users and is not directed at children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
