FAQ

Common Questions.

MCA compliance refers to the filings and requirements that companies and LLPs may need to meet with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Requirements can vary depending on the entity type, filing and applicable rules.

ROC compliance generally refers to filings and information submitted to the Registrar of Companies, such as annual filings. Requirements can vary depending on the entity type, filing and applicable rules.

AOC-4 is a form generally used to file financial statements with the MCA, where applicable to the company. Requirements can vary depending on the entity type, filing and applicable rules.

DIR-3 KYC is a director KYC filing related to directors holding a Director Identification Number, where applicable. Requirements can vary depending on the individual case and applicable rules.

Companies are generally subject to periodic MCA/ROC filing requirements. Requirements can vary depending on the entity type, filing and applicable rules, so please confirm what applies to your company.

LLPs are covered by their own filing and compliance framework, which differs from that of companies. Requirements can vary depending on the entity type, filing and applicable rules.
